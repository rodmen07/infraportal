### 2026-07-29

- **Consulting ladder repriced, and the anti-discount closed** - owner-directed. The base rate moves $125/hr → **$100/hr**, and the bulk packages now actually discount: **$640 / $1,040 / $1,200 per week** at 8 / 16 / 24 hrs/week, i.e. **$80 / $65 / $50 per hour**, so each step up deepens the discount by a uniform 15 points (20% / 35% / 50% off) and the top tier is half the base. Project becomes a fixed **$5,200** 4-week sprint (80 hrs at $65/hr) instead of a $3,400-$5,100 band derived from the old hourly rate. **The defect this fixes:** the prior retainers were authored as hour RANGES ($1,500/week for 10-15 hrs), so their effective rate was a BAND of $100-$150/hr against a $125/hr base. The bulk tiers cost more per hour than the base rate at the top of every band and Premium's floor ($120) sat above Starter's ($100), so the discount ran backwards. Fixed hour counts replace every range.
